## How this was gathered (and its limitations)

- **The site itself could not be scraped directly.** `www.vincereportfolios.com`
  returns HTTP 403 to automated fetchers (bot protection), and this sandboxed
  environment's network policy additionally blocks direct outbound HTTP and
  archive.org. Direct page mirrors (HTML/CSS/images) therefore could not be saved.
- **All content below was reconstructed from web-search indexes** of the site's
  two indexed pages (`/` and `/team`) plus its substantial third-party footprint:
  press releases, sponsored articles, review sites (Trustpilot, BBB, Scamadviser,
  Algo Institute), and company registries (Crunchbase, LinkedIn).
- Quotes and figures are as reported by those sources on the capture date; the
  live site may say more or different things. URLs in `sources.md` allow
  re-capture from an unrestricted machine (e.g. `wget --mirror` with a browser
  user-agent, or the Wayback Machine's "Save Page Now").

## One-paragraph summary

Vincere Portfolios is a Chicago-area (Lake Forest, IL) algorithmic-trading
product marketed to individual investors, operated by Triple V Industries, Inc.
d/b/a Vincere Trading, founded by Alex Cecola. It sells a one-time software
license to a suite of 12+ proprietary algorithms that trade U.S. index futures
(E-mini S&P 500, Nasdaq 100) in the customer's own brokerage or self-directed
IRA account (NinjaTrader / Equity Trust). Marketing claims include ~1400%
compounded outperformance of the S&P 500 since 2020, ~4.4% average monthly
returns, 90.8% profitable months, and a GIPS-verification audit by "Absolute
Performance Verification." Notable counterweights: the company states it is
**not registered with any financial regulatory authority**, the BBB profile
shows the business started **June 2024** (despite the "since 2020" performance
narrative), an independent reviewer (Algo Institute) rates it 4.6/10
"Sub-Standard," and no Trustpilot review page for vincereportfolios.com itself
was found in the search index — only for the sister domain vinceretrading.com
(4★, 81 reviews, domain reported closed).
