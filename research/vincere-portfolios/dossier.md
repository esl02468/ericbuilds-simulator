## 3. Product & how it works (marketing claims)

- Suite of **12+ proprietary, uncorrelated algorithms**, each trading major
  U.S. index futures (E-mini S&P 500, Nasdaq 100) with distinct logic.
- Fully automated execution "based on mathematical models and systematic rules";
  no coding required; built for **NinjaTrader 8, MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5**.
- Trades run in the **customer's own brokerage account** (customer keeps
  custody); a "brokerage integration framework" connects the algorithms to the
  external account for real-time execution.
- Markets: "highly liquid U.S.-regulated futures markets," enabling same-day
  withdrawals and ability to stop at any time; press copy also mentions
  futures across indexes, commodities, currencies, and interest-rate products.
- **Risk-management claims:** no leverage; no overnight positions; every trade
  has a pre-defined maximum loss of **0.27%–3%** of account value.
- **Self-directed IRA path:** partnership/compatibility with **Equity Trust
  Company** — open SD-IRA at Equity Trust → fund via transfer/rollover →
  fund a NinjaTrader futures account from the IRA → license and deploy the
  algorithms; one-on-one installation/configuration by a U.S.-based team.
- The prop-firm sibling (vincerealgorithms.com) markets the same tech at
  passing funded-account evaluations; claims "2–7X return on total prop-firm
  costs within 1–3 years" and "every trader using their algorithms for 12+
  months has generated net profits."

## 4. Performance claims (company-sourced, unverified)

- Since inception in **2020**, algorithms have "outperformed the S&P 500 Index
  by ~1400% compounded, with ~1/7 the maximum one-time loss (drawdown)."
- "~50% average annual growth over approximately six years of audited
  performance"; **4.4% average monthly return**; **90.8% of months since
  January 2020 profitable**.
- Performance history claimed to be audited by **"Absolute Performance
  Verification,"** described as a specialist firm verifying compliance with
  CFA Institute GIPS standards.
- Publishes "6+ years of audited and verified algorithm performance data
  publicly, including drawdown periods."
- ⚠️ Tension worth noting: the six-years-of-performance narrative (since
  Jan 2020) vs. the BBB record showing the business started **June 2024** —
  pre-launch performance is presumably backtested or from a predecessor
  operation; nothing found independently verifies the audit or the auditor.

## 5. Pricing & account requirements

- **Model:** one-time software **license fee** (amount not publicly disclosed
  anywhere found); explicitly no AUM fees and no share of profits.
- **Minimum capital:** $30,000 to participate.
- **Full 12-algorithm suite:** optimized for accounts of **$90,000–$120,000+**.
